# Habits

Habits to prompt during daily and weekly workflows. Each section is read by the corresponding workflow at the relevant trigger point. Fill with your own habits — workflows will surface them as questions/reminders.

## Daily — Morning

| # | Habit | Trigger | Prompt |
|---|-------|---------|--------|
| 1 | Strategic lens | Every day | "Does today's main work advance the team/department, or only my own queue?" If only own queue for 3+ days, find one enabling action. |
| 2 | Unblock check | Every day | "Who could I unblock with 15 minutes?" (a review, a Slack answer, a quick design chat). Do it before deep work. |
| 3 | Decision logging | When priorities shift | If something is deprioritized or deferred, log one line: "Deprioritized: [reason/who directed]." |
| 4 | Mid-week replan | Wednesday | If priorities shifted from Monday's plan, write a one-line replan note. Not to force breadth — to keep the record honest. |
| 5 | Recovery planning | After weekend/holiday work | Plan fewer MITs, protect one afternoon. Don't stack a full day after extra hours. |

## Daily — Evening

| # | Habit | Trigger | Prompt |
|---|-------|---------|--------|
| 1 | End-of-day scan | Every day | Check waiting-for items > 2 working days. If stalled: send specific yes/no question, set a deadline, or escalate. Log: `End-of-day scan: [items actioned / nothing pending].` |
| 2 | Enabling log | Every day | If you unblocked someone, gave design guidance, shared knowledge, or reviewed architecture — log a one-liner. If nothing: that's fine, don't fabricate. |
| 3 | Deprioritization capture | Every day | Any decisions to defer/drop/shift today that aren't logged yet? Capture now. |
| 4 | Signal check | Every day | "Did I learn something today that changes architectural direction or affects another team?" If yes — note it, share tomorrow. |

## Weekly — Monday (Planning)

| # | Habit | Prompt |
|---|-------|--------|
| 1 | Initiative health | For each initiative you launched: is it progressing without you? If stalled, one action to re-energize. |
| 2 | Cross-team scan | What's happening in adjacent teams (via Slack, standup notes, Jira) that affects your team broadly? Note anything requiring action. |
| 3 | Delegation review | "What am I doing this week that someone else could own if I spent 30 minutes teaching them?" Identify one candidate. |
| 4 | Enabling target | Choose one enabling interaction for the week: a review, a pairing session, a Slack write-up, a presentation. |

## Weekly — Friday (Wrap-up)

| # | Habit | Prompt |
|---|-------|--------|
| 1 | Reflection | Two sentences minimum: what the week proved, what to do differently. Non-negotiable. |
| 2 | Knowledge shared? | Did you publish, share, or present something this week? If not for 2+ weeks, plan one for next week. |
| 3 | Weekend hours | Log: "Weekend hours: [n]." If >0 last week, did you take a lighter day? |
| 4 | Drift audit | Any item open >2 weeks without a decision? Apply: defer/drop/delegate. |
| 5 | Enabling tally | Count enabling interactions this week. Target: ≥1. If 0: plan one for next week explicitly. |

## Principles

- **Log decisions, not just work.** Your track record should be readable from artifacts without oral clarification.
- **Enable by default.** Design → enable others to run it → step back. Ask: "What can I hand off?"
- **Protect sustainability.** High engagement masks fatigue. Track extra hours; plan recovery.
- **Stay cross-team aware.** Notice patterns across teams; connect dots others miss.
- **Share proactively.** When you solve something non-trivial, post a short summary. Don't wait to be asked.
